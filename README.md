# Bloodfallen-Releases

![Bloodfallen 0.3.1a gameplay: the Knight cutting through the graveyard horde](media/bloodfallen-teaser-0.3.1a.gif)

Welcome to the Bloodfallen releases repository! Here, you'll find the bleeding edge versions of our game, designed for those who want to dive into the game's development from the ground up.
## What Is Bloodfallen?
Bloodfallen is a top-down, rogue-lite game. We're in the early stages, and your feedback is crucial to shaping the game's future.
## How To Get Involved
- **Download**: Get the latest build from [Releases](https://github.com/Lilium-Legacy-Games/bloodfallen-releases/releases): `bloodfallen-<version>-windows.zip` for Windows, `bloodfallen-<version>-linux.tar.gz` for Linux, `bloodfallen-<version>-android.apk` for Android (open it on your phone to install). From 0.4.0a on, the Windows and Linux versions update themselves from the main menu; on Android the main menu tells you when a new version is out and DOWNLOAD opens its release page, and installing the new APK keeps your progress.
- **Feedback**: Encounter a bug or have a suggestion? Post it in the [Issues](https://github.com/Lilium-Legacy-Games/bloodfallen-releases/issues) section or email us at [liliumlegacygames@gmail.com](mailto:liliumlegacygames@gmail.com).
- **Testing**: Your role as a tester is invaluable. We encourage you to play, break, and report back to help us improve.

## Patch Notes
### Version 0.5.13a
- **New Features**:
  - Mini bosses: two on every level come before its boss, at 5 and 10 minutes in Survival and after waves 3 and 6 in Waves mode. The Graveyard has the Gravedigger and the Headless Knight, the Crypt the Bell-Ringer and the Lantern Keeper, the Dunes the Scarab King and the High Vizier, each with its own look and attacks
  - As a mini boss arrives the crowd is cleared and its health bar comes up; the run's clock waits until it falls. It leaves a gold chest of 5 rewards
  - Waves mode has 9 waves, with the mini bosses after the third and the sixth and the boss after the ninth
  - Ghosts no longer shoot: the Banshee, the Wraith and the Sun Priest are each level's fastest enemy and drift straight through tombstones, crosses and every other obstacle
  - An elite's chest is now always a bronze one with 1 reward, and a mini boss's gold chest looks its own: larger, domed and ornate
  - A chest's rewards can be skipped: a tap, click or any key lands the reward that's spinning and starts the next. Once all are shown they glow, and one more press claims them
- **Bug Fixes**:
  - The Hunter draws his bow after each shot instead of before it, and the arrow on his bow no longer blinks out and back. The Shade and the Blood Witch's attacks are timed the same way
  - The kill count stays on one line on a phone, however many kills

### Version 0.5.12a
- **New Features**:
  - The Blood Moon is new: for half a minute every enemy moves faster, and a shaft of moonlight falls on a pool near you. Standing in it heals you and doubles your damage, and it moves on every few seconds
  - A stampede now charges three times, ten seconds apart, each time from a different side
  - The treasure ghoul stays 40 seconds. When it gets away, it sprints off through the palisade and out of sight instead of vanishing
  - A chest's rewards spin slower and slow down as they land, and stay on screen for 3 seconds
  - The Hunter's Arrow Storm covers a wider spot and leaves the ground burning for 3 seconds, hurting enemies that cross it
  - The Arrow Rain throwable covers a smaller spot
- **Bug Fixes**:
  - The treasure ghoul's marker stays at the screen's edge nearest the ghoul: it no longer jumps across the screen or disappears
  - The treasure ghoul no longer twitches on the spot when you get close: it keeps running, along the palisade if it has to
  - A stampede's warning arrows no longer cover the level-up cards and their reroll buttons
  - A stampede's runners come in from out of sight and run over you, hurting you as they pass, instead of bumping into you

### Version 0.5.11a

  - A treasure ghoul turns up twice a run and runs from you: catch it within 30 seconds for 20 coins. A marker at the screen's edge shows where it is

- **Bug Fixes**:
  - The war horn at the start of a wave is a real battle horn and carries over the music
  - Button labels that barely fit in some languages are shorter
  - When only two upgrades are left, their cards sit in the middle of the screen
  - An achievement's coins no longer count towards what the run earned; they still go into your total
  - The Hunter's longbow is weaker at its top levels: smaller explosions, and the evolved bow fires 3 arrows that pierce 3 enemies
